Five things that actually helped me sleep again

Not a listicle of everything on Amazon with "sleep" in the title. Five products, tested over months of bad nights, that earned a permanent spot on the nightstand.

Ranked by how much they changed a real night's sleep — not by price, star count, or how shiny the packaging is.

Every product here was used for at least three weeks before it made the list — no products were only unboxed and photographed. We paid full price for four of the five; the fifth was sent by the brand for review and is marked below.

We're not sleep doctors. If insomnia is affecting your ability to function, that's worth a conversation with an actual one — this page is for the ordinary bad-sleeper, not a substitute for care.

Questions worth asking before you buy

Do I need all five, or just one?

Start with whichever addresses your specific problem — a weighted blanket for restlessness, blackout shades for a too-bright room, magnesium for a wired nervous system. Layering more than one at a time makes it hard to tell what's actually working.

How long before I'll notice a difference?

Give any single change at least two weeks before judging it. Sleep habits built over years don't undo themselves in one good night, and one bad night doesn't mean the product failed.

What if none of this is enough?

These are conditions worth improving, not treatment. If poor sleep is persistent and affecting your daily life, that's a conversation for a doctor, not a shopping list.
